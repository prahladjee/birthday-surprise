# 🎂 Birthday Surprise Website for Her 💕

A romantic, mobile-friendly animated birthday website ready to host on **GitHub Pages**.

## ✨ What's Included:
1. **Interactive Gift Unboxing Opening Screen**: 3D gift box with popping lid and confetti explosion.
2. **Ambient Floating Hearts & Sparkles**: Continuous smooth canvas animation.
3. **Interactive 3D Birthday Cake**: Blow out candles one by one or all at once with smoke & confetti bursts.
4. **Polaroid Memory Gallery**: Realistic Polaroid cards with washi tape, custom captions, dates, and full-screen lightbox zoom.
5. **Flip Cards ("Little Things I Adore About You")**: 3D flip effect revealing sweet reasons and love notes.
6. **Handwritten Parchment Love Letter**: Romantic letter styling with wax seal.
7. **Music Box Melody / Background Song**: Plays a gentle music-box tune automatically or your own MP3.
8. **Celebration Fireworks**: Full-screen firework particle cannon.

---

## 📸 How to Add Her Real Photos

1. Put your photos into the `assets/images/` folder:
   - `assets/images/photo1.jpg`
   - `assets/images/photo2.jpg`
   - `assets/images/photo3.jpg`
   - `assets/images/photo4.jpg`
2. In `index.html`, simply update the `src` attribute of the polaroid cards:
   ```html
   <img src="assets/images/photo1.jpg" alt="Memory 1" class="polaroid-img" id="img-0">
   ```
3. Change the text in captions to your special dates and inside jokes!

---

## 🎵 (Optional) How to Add Your Song
Place an MP3 file named `music.mp3` inside `assets/`. The website will automatically use it!

---

## 🚀 How to Host on GitHub Pages (Free in 2 Minutes)

1. Open [GitHub.com](https://github.com) and create a new repository (e.g. `birthday-surprise`). Make it **Public**.
2. Upload the files (`index.html`, `style.css`, `script.js`, and the `assets` folder).
3. On GitHub, go to your repository **Settings** -> **Pages** (on the left menu).
4. Under **Branch**, select `main` (or `master`) and click **Save**.
5. Within 1 minute, GitHub will give you a live link like:
   `https://<your-username>.github.io/birthday-surprise/`
6. Send the link to your girlfriend at midnight! 🎉
